# Lick Paw Animation Strip

Create one coherent horizontal row containing exactly 8 separated full-body poses of the canonical tabby cat from `lihua-idle-v2.png`.

## Identity Lock

- Preserve the exact narrow face, tall ears, yellow-green eyes, forehead M, cheek stripes, warm brown-gray coat, fuller belly, long ringed tail, blue scarf, and small M tag.
- Keep the same polished high-resolution pixel-art rendering, proportions, palette, outline weight, and facing direction in every pose.
- Keep one shared scale, ground baseline, and body registration across the complete row.

## Action Sequence

1. Neutral seated pose with both front paws on the ground.
2. Look down toward the screen-left/front paw and shift weight subtly to the other foreleg.
3. Lift that paw smoothly toward the chest; the elbow bends naturally.
4. Bring the paw to the mouth and tilt the head slightly; tongue first touches the paw.
5. First clear lick stroke across the raised paw.
6. Second lick with a small paw rotation and a slightly different tongue/head position.
7. Retract the tongue and lower the paw toward the ground.
8. Return to the exact neutral seated pose from frame 1.

## Production Constraints

- Exactly 8 poses, left to right, in one horizontal row.
- Flat solid `#FF00FF` chroma-key background across the whole image.
- Complete separated silhouettes with generous spacing; no overlap and no cropping at the outer canvas edges.
- No panel borders, labels, numbers, captions, guide marks, shadows, floor patches, motion lines, sparkles, saliva droplets, or detached effects.
- The tongue must remain physically connected to the mouth and touch the raised paw only in licking frames.
- This is grooming, not waving: the paw stays close to the cat's chest and mouth.
